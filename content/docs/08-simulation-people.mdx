The People domain covers the simulations that read the island through the people on it: how they crowd, how they clear a building, how heat sits on the ground, and how reachable the place is. Each persona below runs the sims relevant to its remit; the same sim can read differently for a different persona, so it is documented per persona here.

The scope label in the tool reads Shura Island as a placeholder and is not load-bearing in these descriptions.

## Guest Experience Manager

The Guest Experience read is the guest's side: where guests build up, how they get out, how the heat feels, and how easily they reach what they came for.
