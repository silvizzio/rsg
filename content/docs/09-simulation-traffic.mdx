The Traffic domain covers movement and the systems that carry it: guest mobility, the shuttle network, the drop-off forecourt, the island microgrid that powers it, and, for Marine Operations, the water-flow reads that govern vessel movement. Each persona below runs the sims relevant to its remit.

The scope label in the tool reads Shura Island as a placeholder and is not load-bearing in these descriptions.
