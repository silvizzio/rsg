Transport simulations cover wind, traffic emissions and noise, conflicts between pedestrians and vehicles, and flooding from storms, tides, and rainfall. The sections below explain the results relevant to each operator role.

The scope label currently shows Shura Island as a placeholder; it does not define the scope of the examples below.

## Wind

The wind model shows speed and direction across the resort and surrounding water.

### Guest Experience Manager

Use the wind simulation to assess comfort on outdoor decks, routes, and gathering spaces. Airflow can accelerate around corners and between buildings, leaving one area exposed while a nearby space remains comfortable.

The forecast shows these local differences under the selected wind conditions. Use it to review building layout, planting, or routes where guests would otherwise be exposed to strong wind.

![Wind, Guest Experience view. Wind picks up on exposed decks and routes, with guests and vehicles in the exposed zone.](03-gxm-lod3-simulation-transport-wind.jpg)

## Emissions and noise

The model shows how emissions and noise spread from their sources across the site.

### Guest Experience Manager

Use this simulation to assess how roads, service areas, and plant affect the air and noise levels in guest spaces. Pay particular attention to paths beside busy roads and gardens intended to be quiet.

The forecast shows how emissions and noise spread from their sources. It helps identify where routing or screening changes could reduce exposure and protect the intended character of those spaces.

![Emissions and noise, Guest Experience view. Noise near the plaza climbs at peak traffic, with guests exposed along the roads.](03-gxm-lod3-simulation-transport-emission.jpg)

### Environment Manager

The Environment Manager uses this simulation to assess emissions and noise across the whole site. It shows where exposure accumulates and where it dissipates under the selected conditions.

Review the affected areas alongside their sources. This helps direct mitigation towards the sources contributing most to the problem and towards areas intended to remain clean and quiet.

![Emissions and noise, Environment view.](05-env-lod3-simulation-transport-emission-and-noise.jpg)

## Safety conflicts

The safety model identifies crossing points and shared spaces where pedestrian and vehicle movements may conflict.

### Guest Experience Manager

Use this simulation to identify where guest and vehicle routes intersect and could create a safety conflict. The focus is on specific crossing points and shared spaces.

The forecast ranks those locations by the frequency and severity of the conflicts. Use the results to review crossings, signage, and separation where the model indicates the greatest risk.

![Safety conflicts, Guest Experience view. The plaza crossing reaches conflict level at peak, with people and vehicles exposed.](03-gxm-lod3-simulation-transport-safety.jpg)

### Operations Manager

Operations uses this simulation to identify crossings and shared spaces that may need staff or temporary controls during busy periods.

The forecast locates and ranks potential conflicts between pedestrians and vehicles. Review the highest-priority locations when planning staffing, signage, and temporary traffic control.

![Safety conflicts, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-transport-safety.jpg)

## Flood and storm surge

The flood model shows how storms, tides and rainfall affect water levels on land and at the marina.

### Operations Manager

Use this simulation to assess flood exposure at critical facilities and assets under a selected storm, tide, and rainfall scenario. It helps the team plan what needs to be secured or cleared before the water arrives.

The forecast shows how far the water reaches, its depth at affected facilities, and how its arrival compares with evacuation timing. Use those results to plan the response while there is still time to move people and assets.

![Flood and storm surge, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-transport-flood-and-storm-surge.jpg)

### Environment Manager

The Environment Manager uses this simulation to assess habitats in the path of a flood or storm surge. It considers both inundation and the movement of sediment through sensitive areas.

The forecast shows where water reaches, how levels change, and which ecosystems are exposed. Use it to plan protection and post-event recovery around the areas likely to be most affected.

![Flood and storm surge, Environment view.](05-env-lod3-simulation-transport-flood-and-storm-surge.jpg)

### Marine Operations Manager

Use this simulation to assess the effect of a storm, tide, and rainfall scenario on vessels and the marina. The aim is to plan when craft should be secured or relocated and when barriers should be raised.

The forecast shows surge height at the berths, water speed, and arrival time. Compare those conditions with the time needed to prepare the marina and move vessels before the peak.

![Flood and storm surge, Marine Operations view. Surge at the marina 1.8m, 210 guests and 24 vehicles in the depth zone, surge arrival 18 min.](06-mo-lod3-simulation-flood-and-storm-surge.jpg)
