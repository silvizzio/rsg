The Environment domain covers the environmental simulations: water quality and clarity, ocean current, wave energy, coral reef heat stress, and, for Marine Operations, wind over the water. Each persona below runs the sims relevant to its remit.

The scope label in the tool reads Shura Island as a placeholder and is not load-bearing in these descriptions.
