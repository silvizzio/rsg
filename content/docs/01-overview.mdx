The RSG Integrated Operations Centre (IOC) Digital Twin is Vizzio's live 3D view of Red Sea Global destinations. It brings guest movements, transport, environmental conditions, and marine activity together on one interactive map, giving teams a shared view of what is happening.

Four operator roles use the IOC: Guest Experience Manager, Operations Manager, Environment Manager, and Marine Operations Manager. Each role, called a persona in this guide, has its own metrics, cameras, and alerts. All four use the same map and can move from a destination overview to district and site detail. The map runs on Mapbox GL JS.

![The IOC at startup: Shura Island in the Guest Experience Manager view, map LOD 1.](03-gxm-lod1-1.jpg)

## What the IOC does

Sign in with the account assigned to your role to see the relevant destination data. From the overview, select a district or site to inspect local conditions, view live closed-circuit television (CCTV) feeds, and respond to alerts. Simulation mode lets you explore what could happen under a different set of conditions.

Metrics, camera feeds, and alerts are linked to locations on the map. This lets you check where a problem is developing and compare the information about that place before deciding what to do.

## One twin, two modes

The IOC has two modes: Live and Simulation. Both use the same map, four personas, and three levels of detail (LOD 1, 2, and 3). Live mode shows current conditions on Shura Island using detections, telemetry, and CCTV feeds.

![Live mode: the Operations Manager HQ view, reading the destination as it is right now.](04-ops-lod3-hq.jpg)

Simulation mode starts with the destination's live state and models a selected scenario, such as increased arrivals, fewer available vehicles, or a change in wind over the reef. In the default state, you set up the scenario. Once the run starts, the twin displays the forecast.

![Simulation mode: a crowd run in the Guest Experience Manager view, the same twin carried forward under a chosen scenario.](03-gxm-lod3-simulation-people-crowd.jpg)

The Persona Views chapters explain Live mode for each role. The Simulation chapters cover scenario setup, forecast results, and the specialist engines behind them.

## Personas

Each persona focuses on a different part of destination operations. Together they cover the guest journey, transport, environmental conditions, and marine activity. The table uses the working role titles used throughout this guide; their formal expansions should be confirmed against the RSG IOC Technical statement of work.

| Persona | Expansion | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Guest Experience Manager | Guest Experience Management | Destination-wide executive view, guest flow, experience KPIs and alerts |
| Operations Manager | Operations | Live incident management, CCTV grid, dispatch, operational alerts |
| Environment Manager | Environment | Air quality, weather, regenerative and conservation metrics |
| Marine Operations Manager | Marine Operations | Vessel and marine traffic, dive sites, coastal and marine alerts |

## Map levels of detail

The map has three levels of detail, referred to as map LOD. As you move between them, the map and its associated metrics change to match the area you select.

- **Map LOD 1, destination overview:** the whole destination, with combined KPIs from all active layers. Use it to identify patterns across the destination.
- **Map LOD 2, district zoom:** a selected district or zone, with local metrics, status, and neighbouring zones. Use it to find the area behind a destination-wide change.
- **Map LOD 3, site detail:** a single site or asset, with live feeds and recommended actions. Use it to investigate conditions and review a response.
