The Operations Manager view helps you monitor the fleet and address breakdowns, delays, and idle capacity before they lead to missed transfers or complaints.

The view covers fleet availability, vehicle utilisation, driver safety and compliance, and EV charging. Read these together to see what is in service, where capacity is under pressure, and which vehicles or chargers need attention.

![The wide view of the destination. The bottom strip is the operations summary: fleet and maintenance, utilization, driver safety, and EV charging.](04-ops-lod1-1.jpg)

### Evacuation and bottleneck severity

The building model includes a fire-evacuation simulation using Pathfinder. The left panel shows the percentage evacuated, estimated clearance time against the ASET margin, route throughput per minute, and the number of people waiting at bottlenecks.

Each bottleneck appears as a card with a severity tier, location, density and threshold, projected impact, and recommended action. Review Alert cards first, followed by Critical and Warning cards.

**Alert.** Flow at a chokepoint is near zero and the queue is backing into the space behind it. Review this tier first because people continue to accumulate upstream.

![Alert: movement through a doorway has stalled, and the queue is backing up behind it.](04-ops-lod3-bim-2-human-evacuation-bottleneck-alert.jpg)

**Critical.** Flow is approaching a hard limit but has not stopped. In this example, people from two floors merge into a stairwell near its descent capacity. Review the merge before it becomes an Alert.

![Critical: people from two floors merge into a stairwell that is approaching its descent capacity.](04-ops-lod3-bim-2-human-evacuation-bottleneck-critical.jpg)

**Warning.** Density is rising while people can still move through the space. Monitor the trend and check the flow details for signs that it is approaching Critical.

![Warning: corridor density is rising while people are still moving.](04-ops-lod3-bim-2-human-evacuation-bottleneck-warning.jpg)

| Tier | Trigger | What is happening | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alert | Density past the crush band (5.4 against 4.0 pax/m2) | Egress near zero at the chokepoint, the queue backing into the corridor | Inspect this node |
| Critical | Approaching a hard limit (82 against 85%) | Descent flow nearing stair capacity, a merge conflict projected next interval | Isolate the merge point |
| Warning | Density above the comfortable line (2.8 against 2.5 pax/m2) | Flow still moving, density elevated and trending toward Critical | Check flow detail |

## Simulations this view runs

Operations simulations assess crowd and evacuation capacity, fleet and microgrid demand, and hazards that may require a response. The simulation object is Humans and Vehicles. The table summarises the available simulations, with fuller explanations below and in the linked chapters.

| Category | Simulation | What it shows | Decisions it supports | Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| People | Crowd | Gate capacity and staffing against footfall | Position staff and open lanes before an entrance gridlocks | [Crowd](/docs/08-simulation-people#crowd__operations-manager) |
| People | Evacuation | Clearance time and the exit limiting flow | Review the exit or route that limits clearance | [Evacuation](/docs/08-simulation-people#evacuation__operations-manager) |
| Traffic | Mobility | The day's load against fleet and charging | Identify the capacity shortfall and rebalance before the peak | [Mobility](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#mobility__operations-manager) |
| Traffic | Energy | Renewable share, backup trigger, and reserve | Plan dispatch and reserve to address a supply gap | [Energy](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#energy__operations-manager) |
| Traffic | Shuttle network | Utilisation and routes approaching capacity | Add or rebalance service on busy routes | [Shuttle network](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#shuttle-network__operations-manager) |
| Traffic | Drop-off forecourt | Throughput through kerb, bays, and staff | Position staff and manage the kerb ahead of the rush | [Drop-off forecourt](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#drop-off-forecourt__operations-manager) |
| Transport | Safety conflicts | Crossings where conflicts increase during busy periods | Focus staffing and control where risk concentrates | [Safety conflicts](/docs/10-simulation-transport#safety-conflicts__operations-manager) |
| Transport | Flood and storm surge | Surge reach and depth at critical facilities | Secure assets and clear facilities before the surge arrives | [Flood and storm surge](/docs/10-simulation-transport#flood-and-storm-surge__operations-manager) |

### People

**Crowd.** For Operations, the crowd simulation checks whether entrances and staffing can handle the expected footfall. It shows which gates are likely to reach capacity first and where queues could block entry. Compare the forecast with the available staff and lanes. Use it to plan where to position people and open extra lanes before the busiest period. [Read in full](/docs/08-simulation-people#crowd__operations-manager)

![Crowd, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-people-crowd.jpg)

**Evacuation.** Operations uses the evacuation simulation to compare the estimated clearance time with the site's target and available safety margin. It shows how people move through the building, where flow slows, and which exits carry the greatest load. Check the route or exit that limits the overall clearance rate. That is where a change could have the greatest effect on evacuation time. The result supports planning and review; it describes the modelled scenario rather than guaranteeing how an incident will unfold. [Read in full](/docs/08-simulation-people#evacuation__operations-manager)

![Evacuation, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-people-evacuation.jpg)

### Traffic

**Mobility.** Operations uses the mobility simulation to check whether the available fleet and charging network can meet the day's demand. Both matter: a vehicle must be in service and have enough charge to carry out its trips. The forecast identifies whether vehicle availability, charging capacity, or both will constrain service at the peak. Review that shortfall before deciding how to rebalance the fleet and charging schedule. [Read in full](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#mobility__operations-manager)

![Mobility, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-traffic-mobility.jpg)

**Energy.** Use the energy simulation to compare the island's electricity demand with its renewable supply and reserves. Watch for a supply gap that could trigger diesel backup. The forecast shows the renewable share over time and the reserve available to cover a shortfall. Use these results to review dispatch, EV charging, and reserve choices before demand reaches its peak. [Read in full](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#energy__operations-manager)

![Energy, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-traffic-energy.jpg)

**Shuttle network.** Operations uses this simulation to compare demand with the capacity of each shuttle route. It helps identify heavily used legs and uneven fleet utilisation that could reduce reliability. Check which routes approach capacity during the forecast period. Use the results to consider adding service or reallocating vehicles before those routes become overloaded. [Read in full](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#shuttle-network__operations-manager)

![Shuttle network, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-traffic-shuttle-network.jpg)

**Drop-off forecourt.** Operations uses the forecourt simulation to check how quickly vehicles can pass through the kerbside bays with the staff available. The concern is a queue extending from the forecourt into the approach road. Compare the arrival pattern with the forecourt's capacity and watch where vehicle staging falls behind. Use the forecast to plan staff positions and kerb management for the expected peak. [Read in full](/docs/09-simulation-traffic#drop-off-forecourt__operations-manager)

![Drop-off forecourt, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-traffic-drop-off-forecourt.jpg)

### Transport

**Safety conflicts.** Operations uses this simulation to identify crossings and shared spaces that may need staff or temporary controls during busy periods. The forecast locates and ranks potential conflicts between pedestrians and vehicles. Review the highest-priority locations when planning staffing, signage, and temporary traffic control. [Read in full](/docs/10-simulation-transport#safety-conflicts__operations-manager)

![Safety conflicts, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-transport-safety.jpg)

**Flood and storm surge.** Use this simulation to assess flood exposure at critical facilities and assets under a selected storm, tide, and rainfall scenario. It helps the team plan what needs to be secured or cleared before the water arrives. The forecast shows how far the water reaches, its depth at affected facilities, and how its arrival compares with evacuation timing. Use those results to plan the response while there is still time to move people and assets. [Read in full](/docs/10-simulation-transport#flood-and-storm-surge__operations-manager)

![Flood and storm surge, Operations view.](04-ops-lod3-simulation-transport-flood-and-storm-surge.jpg)

## Working in this view

1. Sign in to the Operations Manager view using your assigned account.
2. Read the destination operations strip at the wide view.
3. Select a hub to see its fleet, utilisation, safety, and EV figures.
4. Open a site to read its recommended action and dispatch a response.
5. Open the site close-up for the consolidated panel, the building model, and the CCTV virtual tour preview.
